# 🌾 PROJECT KISAN — Your AI-Powered Farming Assistant

**Revolutionizing Indian agriculture with intelligent crop diagnosis, real-time market prices, and personalized farming guidance.**  
👉 Live Demo: [projectkisan.vercel.app](https://projectkisan.vercel.app/)

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Crop Diagnosis** – Detect issues and get instant recommendations.  
- 📊 **Farm Analytics** – Visualize soil, crop, and market data with **Chart.js**.  
- 💹 **Real-Time Market Prices** – Stay updated with commodity rates.  
- 🌦 **Weather Forecast** – Integrated with **OpenWeatherMap** for accurate predictions.  
- 🧠 **AI Chatbot** – Smart assistant for farmers to solve queries.  
- 📌 **Smart Alerts & Health Advisory** – Timely notifications for crop health and weather.  
- 📤 **Export Module** – Export reports and insights.  
- 🗺 **Interactive Map** – Built with **Leaflet** for geographical data visualization.  
- 🔒 **Secure Authentication** – Powered by **Supabase Auth**.  
- 🏭 **Government Schemes & Farming Tips** – Stay informed with the latest resources.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons  
- **Backend & Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth)  
- **APIs Integrated:**  
  - [IQAir API](https://www.iqair.com/) – Air Quality Data  
  - [WAQI API](https://aqicn.org/api/) – Global Air Quality Index  
  - [OpenWeatherMap](https://openweathermap.org/) – Weather Forecast  
- **Data Visualization:** Chart.js, Leaflet  
- **AI Features:** Intelligent Analytics, AI Chatbot
